## FAQ Content

**Q: Do you accept walk-ins?**
A: We recommend booking appointments in advance, but we welcome walk-ins based on availability.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept cash, all major credit cards, and digital payments including Apple Pay and Google Pay.

**Q: Do you offer group bookings?**
A: Yes, we offer special rates for groups of 4 or more. Perfect for bridal parties or special occasions.

### Service Questions

**Q: How long does a hair color service take?**
A: Hair coloring typically takes 2-3 hours depending on the desired result and hair length.

**Q: Are your products halal-certified?**
A: Yes, we use halal-certified and cruelty-free products whenever possible.

**Q: Do you provide makeup for special events?**
A: Absolutely! We specialize in bridal, party, and special event makeup with on-location services available.
